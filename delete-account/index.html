Delete your BiteBeat account

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Option 1: Delete inside the app (instant)

  1. Open BiteBeat and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your session ends right away and your account is removed from the app.

Option 2: Request deletion by email (no app needed)

If you uninstalled the app or cannot open it, email us from the address linked to your account:

  1. Send an email to support@skushot.com.
  2. Use the subject BiteBeat account deletion request.
  3. Include the Google account email you used to sign in to BiteBeat.

We may reply to confirm the request is yours before deleting anything. We will confirm once your account is deleted.

Email requests are for accounts signed in with Google only. A guest account (not linked to Google) has no email on file, so it cannot be requested by email. To delete a guest account, use Option 1 inside the app.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and sign-in (Google account link)
  • Meal logs and meal photos
  • Your goals and targets
  • Step, water and sleep data synced to your BiteBeat account
  • Credits history and device installation identifiers

This data is erased from our active databases within 30 calendar days of your request.

What may be kept

  • Records we must keep by tax or commercial bookkeeping law, if any, for the legal retention period. They are then deleted.
  • Data in routine backups is overwritten on the normal backup cycle.

Steps and sleep stored in Health Connect on your phone are not stored by BiteBeat. You can manage or remove them in the Health Connect app.

Questions

Contact support@skushot.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
