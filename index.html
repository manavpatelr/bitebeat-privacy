1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

Account & identity information

  • Name, email address, profile photo link and a unique account ID from Google Sign-In
  • In guest mode: a random installation ID, with no name or email
  • Device identifiers sent to our server: a device ID (Android ID) or installation ID, your time zone and your platform (Android or iOS), used to keep your session and guest account tied to your device and to prevent fraud

Profile you enter

  • Age range, gender, height, weight, bedtime, and hydration and step goals, all self-reported

Meals, activity and credits

  • Meal photos you take or pick, the time, meal title and estimated nutrition values
  • Steps and sleep duration (see section 3), water you log, and which in-app actions you complete
  • Your scan credit balance, credit history and the ads you watch to earn credits

Ads and diagnostics data collected by Google AdMob

  • Advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device model, OS version, ad interactions and diagnostics

Feedback you send

  • If you choose to send feedback, your email app opens with the app version and device type pre-filled. We receive only what you send.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, calendar, microphone recordings or files. We do not read heart rate, blood pressure, blood glucose or other medical records.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Sign you in, secure your account and sync your records
  • Analyze your meals and show insights, daily summaries and streaks
  • Show your activity and sleep, and your progress toward your goals
  • Give and track credits, show rewarded ads and prevent misuse
  • Respond to support requests and keep the App stable and secure

3. Steps and Sleep (Health Data)

  • We read only steps and sleep, from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS), and only after you give permission. We never write to or change your health records.
  • We use this data only to show you your own activity and sleep inside BiteBeat.
  • We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with ad networks or data brokers. We do not use it to judge credit or lending.
  • BiteBeat’s use of information received from Health Connect follows the Google Play Health Connect permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect (Android) or Settings → Health (iOS).

4. Meal Photos & AI Processing

  • An on-device check (Google ML Kit) first confirms the picture shows food. A photo that is not food is not uploaded.
  • A confirmed photo is sent over HTTPS to our server, which uses the Google Gemini API to estimate food and nutrition values.
  • We do not use your meal photos or logs to train public or foundation AI models.
  • Photos are stored privately against your account and removed when you delete the meal or your account.

5. Permissions the App Asks For

  • Camera: to take photos of your meals. You choose when to scan, and you can turn this off in your phone’s settings.
  • Physical activity (step counter): for the live “Steps today” notification and your step count.
  • Notifications: for water, steps and recovery reminders and the live steps notification. These are scheduled on your phone, and you can turn them off in the app or in your phone’s settings.
  • Run after restart: so reminders keep working after you restart your phone.
  • Health Connect: steps and sleep, as described above.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Free users can choose to watch rewarded ads to earn extra scan credits. The App also shows other ads, such as when you open the app and at natural exit points.
  • Google AdMob may use your advertising ID, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data in ads.
  • To confirm a rewarded ad, our server gives AdMob your account ID and a one-time code (server-side verification). We never give AdMob your steps, sleep, meal photos or nutrition data.
  • Where required, the App asks for ad consent. You can change your ad choices in the app under Profile → Privacy Centre → Ad privacy choices, and reset your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

7. How We Share Information

  • Service providers that process data for us: Google (sign-in, Gemini AI, AdMob) and our hosting provider.
  • Legal reasons: when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and users.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and data at any time:

  • In the app: open Profile → Settings → Delete Account and confirm.
  • By email: if you cannot open the app, email support@skushot.com from the address linked to your Google account. Email requests are for accounts signed in with Google. A guest account has no email on file, so delete it inside the app.

See our account deletion page for the full steps. Your profile, sign-in link, meal logs and photos, goals, synced steps and sleep, credits history and installation identifiers are erased from our active systems within 30 days. We may keep records that the law requires us to keep, for the legally required period. Routine backups are overwritten on the normal backup cycle.

9. Security

  • Data travels between the App and our servers over HTTPS.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in your device’s secure storage.

10. Children’s Privacy

BiteBeat is for people aged 13 and over (16 in the EU and UK). We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live (including India’s DPDP Act, the GDPR and the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a regulator. Edit your profile in the App, delete your account as described above, or contact us to use a right.

12. International Transfers

Your data is processed and stored on servers operated for us, and Google processes data to provide sign-in, AI analysis and ads. These servers may be in India or other countries. By using the App you understand that your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We use service providers that protect data as this policy describes.

13. Changes to This Policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date, and tell you in the App if a change is significant. Premium plans are not available yet. If we add paid plans, we will update this policy to explain how purchases and refunds are handled, which are processed by Google Play.
