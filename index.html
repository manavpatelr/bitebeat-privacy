BiteBeat Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

Wellness app, not a medical device

BiteBeat is a lifestyle and wellness tracker. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent or monitor any disease or medical condition. For medical advice, talk to a qualified healthcare provider.

1. About this policy

This policy applies to the BiteBeat mobile app (formerly called HeartMirror), published by Yudiz Solutions Limited, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). It explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

3. Steps and sleep (Health Connect and Apple Health)

  • We only read steps and sleep, and only with your permission. We never write to or change your health records.
  • We use this data only to show you your own activity and sleep inside BiteBeat.
  • We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with ad networks or data brokers. We do not use it to judge credit or lending.
  • BiteBeat's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Google Play Health Connect permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect (Android) or Settings → Health (iOS).

3a. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to take photos of your meals. You choose when to scan. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.
  • Physical activity (step counter): for the live "Steps today" notification and your step count.
  • Notifications: for water, steps and recovery reminders and the live steps notification. These are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Run after restart: so reminders keep working after you restart your phone.
  • Health Connect: steps and sleep, as described above.

4. Meal photos and AI processing

  • An on-device check (Google ML Kit) first confirms the picture shows food. A photo that is not food is not uploaded.
  • A confirmed photo is sent over HTTPS to our server, which uses the Google Gemini API to estimate food and nutrition values.
  • We do not use your meal photos or logs to train public or foundation AI models.
  • Photos are stored privately against your account and removed when you delete the meal or your account.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Free users can choose to watch rewarded ads to earn extra scan credits. The app also shows other ads, such as when you open the app and at natural exit points.
  • Google AdMob may use your advertising ID, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data in ads.
  • To confirm a rewarded ad, our server gives AdMob your account ID and a one-time code (server-side verification). We never give AdMob your steps, sleep, meal photos or nutrition data.
  • Where required, the app asks for ad consent. You can change your ad choices in the app under Profile → Privacy Centre → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

6. Who we share data with

  • Service providers that process data for us: Google (sign-in, Gemini AI, AdMob) and our hosting provider.
  • Legal reasons: when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and users.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

6a. Where your data is processed

Your data is processed and stored on servers operated for us, and Google processes data to provide sign-in, AI analysis and ads. These servers may be in India or other countries. By using the app you understand that your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We use service providers that protect data as this policy describes.

7. Delete your account and data

You can also read these steps on our account deletion page.

In the app: open Profile → Settings → Delete Account and confirm.

By email: if you cannot open the app, email support@skushot.com from the address linked to your Google account, with the subject "BiteBeat account deletion request". Email requests are for accounts signed in with Google. A guest account has no email on file, so delete it inside the app.

Your profile, sign-in link, meal logs and photos, goals, synced steps and sleep, credits history and installation identifiers are erased from our active systems within 30 days. We may keep records that the law requires us to keep, for the legally required period. Routine backups are overwritten on the normal backup cycle.

8. Security and retention

  • Data travels between the app and our servers over HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage.
  • We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete it, we remove the data as described above.

9. Children

BiteBeat is for people aged 13 and over (16 in the EU and UK). We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including India's DPDP Act, the GDPR and the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a regulator. Edit your profile in the app, delete your account as above, or contact us to use a right.

11. Changes to this policy

Premium plans are not available yet. If we add paid plans, we will update this policy to explain how purchases and refunds are handled, which are processed by Google Play. If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
