BiteBeat
Android app

Your heart sees every bite.

BiteBeat is a friendly lifestyle heart coach. Snap a photo of your meal and see how your everyday food, movement, sleep and water support your heart wellness. Built for Indian food.

Get it on Google Play

What BiteBeat does

📸 Scan your thali

Take a photo of your plate. BiteBeat recognises Indian dishes like dal, roti, rice and sabzi and shows how the meal supports your heart. No calorie counting.

🫀 A heart that reacts

Your animated heart responds to your daily habits, so you can see the effect of better choices at a glance.

🚶 Daily habits

Track steps, sleep and hydration alongside your meals to build a steady heart-friendly routine.

📈 Weekly insights

See your best days, habit trends and simple tips for the week ahead. Kind and clear, never shaming.

Free and Premium

BiteBeat is free to download. Free accounts scan meals using scan credits, which you can earn by watching short ads.

BiteBeat Premium gives unlimited meal scans, no ads and detailed weekly insights. It is available monthly or yearly through Google Play, and new subscribers can start with a free trial on the monthly plan. You can cancel any time in Google Play.

Wellness app, not a medical device. BiteBeat is a lifestyle and wellness tracker. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent or monitor any disease or medical condition. For medical advice, talk to a qualified healthcare provider.
